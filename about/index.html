About KPG

Kuwait's Interior, Fitout And Construction Partner Built As One Team, Not Three

Kuwait Premium Group was founded on a simple frustration: clients should not have to coordinate separate designers, contractors and specialist trades to deliver one space.

Our integrated team brings design development, technical coordination, fitout and construction together under one accountable delivery structure. Decisions stay connected from the first brief through procurement, site execution and final handover.

That approach gives residential and commercial clients clearer communication, stronger quality control and fewer surprises throughout the programme.

KPG One vision. One coordinated team.

What Sets KPG Apart In Kuwait

  • Craftsmanship led by a coordinated local delivery team
  • One team from concept and technical planning through handover
  • Practical programmes agreed before site work begins
  • Clear communication, documented approvals and controlled changes

KPG By The Numbers

  • Kuwait-focused delivery and aftercare
