Popular queries

Quick And Clear Answers To Your Key Questions

01What interior design services do you offer?

We plan and deliver functional residential and commercial interiors, from initial concepts and space planning through finishes, technical coordination and site execution.

02What fitout services do you offer?

KPG coordinates partitions, ceilings, flooring, finishes, joinery and MEP requirements as one controlled fitout package.

03What is your design process?

We begin with the brief and site information, develop layouts and visual direction, coordinate technical details, approve materials and then proceed to execution.

04How do you establish project fees?

Fees and project costs are based on the agreed scope, area, programme, finish level and specialist requirements.

05Will I need approvals for my project?

Requirements vary by property and project type. We identify relevant approvals during scope definition and coordinate the agreed submissions.

06How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on size, approvals and procurement. A realistic programme is agreed before execution begins.

07Can I request a custom design?

Yes. Each KPG project is tailored to the client, site, operational needs and intended level of finish.
